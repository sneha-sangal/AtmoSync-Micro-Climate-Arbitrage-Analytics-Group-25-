# AtmoSync - Project Findings

## 1. Project Overview
AtmoSync focuses on micro-climate monitoring, shipment analysis, product price comparison, and potential arbitrage opportunities.

## 2. Key Findings

### Shipment Analysis
- Total shipments analyzed: 500
- Bananas recorded the highest shipment count with 113 shipments.
- Shipment data helps understand product movement and transportation patterns.

### Price Comparison
- Total same-day price comparisons: 522
- Comparisons cover 5 products across 12 cities.
- Price differences indicate potential arbitrage opportunities.

### Gross Margin Analysis
- Records with matching route and historical shipment cost data: 212
- Positive estimated gross margins: 193
- Negative estimated gross margins: 19
- Records without matching cost data: 310
- Average estimated gross margin: ₹11.61/kg
- Maximum estimated gross margin: ₹81.16/kg

### Container and Sensor Analysis
- Total containers analyzed: 100
- Sensor readings include temperature, humidity, and vibration.
- These readings help understand container environmental conditions.

### Route Analysis
- Route cost analysis contains 58 records.
- Minimum and maximum estimated route costs help compare transportation scenarios.

## 3. Important Observations
- Product prices vary across cities.
- Shipment and route costs are important for evaluating potential opportunities.
- Sensor readings provide useful information about container conditions.
- Cost data is not available for every price comparison.

## 4. Conclusion
The visualizations provide an overview of shipment patterns, price differences, container conditions, and estimated gross margins.

## 5. Disclaimer
Estimated gross margins are analytical estimates based on available data and should not be considered confirmed profits.
