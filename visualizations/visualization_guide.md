# AtmoSync - Data Visualization Guide

## Overview
This guide explains the 12 visualizations created for the AtmoSync Micro-Climate Arbitrage Analytics project.

## Visualizations

1. **Price Gap by Product**  
   Compares the average price gap across different products.

2. **Positive vs Negative Margins**  
   Shows the distribution of positive and negative estimated gross margins.

3. **Product-wise Gross Margin**  
   Compares average estimated gross margins across products.

4. **Shipment Count by Product**  
   Displays the number of shipments for each product.

5. **Top 10 Price Opportunities**  
   Highlights the top 10 price opportunities.

6. **City-wise Product Price Heatmap**  
   Compares product prices across different cities.

7. **Route Cost Comparison**  
   Compares minimum and maximum estimated route costs.

8. **Container Capacity Analysis**  
   Shows container counts and minimum, average, and maximum capacity.

9. **Sensor Performance Analysis**  
   Presents temperature, humidity, and vibration readings across containers.

10. **Shipment Analysis**  
    Shows transport mode distribution, popular routes, and expected transit days.

11. **Same-day Price Comparison**  
    Compares same-day product prices and potential price gaps.

12. **Final Project Overview**  
    Summarizes key project metrics, including shipments, price comparisons, containers, and route records.

## Purpose
These visualizations help understand shipment patterns, sensor performance, price differences, and potential arbitrage opportunities.

## Note
Estimated gross margins are analytical estimates and should not be treated as confirmed profits.
Key Data Insights

1. Shipment Insights

- Total shipment records: 500
- Bananas have the highest shipment count with 113 shipments.
- The dataset covers five different products.

2. Price Comparison Insights

- A total of 522 same-day product and city price comparisons were identified.
- These comparisons help highlight potential price differences across markets.

3. Gross Margin Insights

- Out of 522 price comparisons, 212 had matching route and historical shipment cost data.
- 193 records showed positive estimated gross margins.
- 19 records showed negative estimated gross margins.
- The average estimated gross margin across these 212 records was ₹11.61 per kg.
- The highest estimated gross margin was ₹81.16 per kg.

4. Data Availability

- 310 price comparisons did not have matching cost data.
- Therefore, their estimated gross margins could not be calculated.

5. Route and Container Insights

- Route cost analysis contains 58 records.
- The container dataset covers 100 containers.
- Route costs and container conditions help provide additional context for the analysis.

Conclusion

The analysis highlights potential market price differences and estimated gross margin opportunities. However, these estimates depend on available cost data and should not be considered confirmed profits.
